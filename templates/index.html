      Built for people who struggle to read screens

      Drishti helps people with low vision, and anyone who finds dense visuals hard to follow.

      • Text, read out

        OCR pulls the words out of timetables, menus and posters so you can read or hear them.

      • Plain-language explanation

        With an AI key set up, Drishti describes the layout and says what to pay attention to.

      • Honest labels

        Every result says whether it came from OCR or AI, so you know what to double-check.

      • Your display, your way

        Larger text, high contrast, image magnifier, and adjustable speech speed and voice.
